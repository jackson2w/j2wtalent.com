About

Willie Jackson

I started as an IT consultant. Enterprise software deployments at Accenture, then years building digital platforms and learning infrastructure. I kept gravitating toward a harder problem, though: how do you get people to actually change? How do you build organizations where that change sticks?

That question led me to Seth Godin's altMBA, where I served as Founding Technical Lead and helped build a global leadership program now reaching 7,000+ professionals across 100+ countries. It led me to found Abernathy, a magazine dedicated to Black leadership and identity, and eventually to ReadySet. I spent seven years there leading inclusive leadership programs for 200+ organizations worldwide, ultimately as Partner and Head of Growth.

In 2015, I launched Jackson Talent Strategies to focus entirely on the work I care most about: helping executives and HR leaders design strategies that actually move people. No junior team between us. No off-the-shelf curriculum. Just rigorous, relational work built around what you're specifically navigating.

Today I partner with organizations on AI upskilling, leadership development, and large-scale organizational change. Based in Dallas, working with clients globally.

The Process

How I Work

01

Start With a Conversation

Before anything else, we talk directly. You describe what's happening, what's been tried, and what success would actually look like. No pitch deck. No intake form. Just a real conversation.

02

Diagnose Before Prescribing

Through interviews, observations, and review of existing data, I work to understand the real problem, not the presenting one. Most organizations know something is wrong. Fewer know exactly what.

03

Design the Engagement

Together, we define the scope, format, and timeline. Engagements are tailored, not templated. Some are six-week intensive sprints. Others are 18-month partnerships. The work dictates the structure.

04

Work Alongside Your Team

I'm in the room, leading offsites, coaching leaders weekly, facilitating difficult conversations. The work happens in direct relationship with your people, not from the outside looking in.

Selected Work

Case Studies

01

Grant-Making Non-Profit · Executive Team & Manager Development

Rebuilding Leadership Alignment After an Unexpected CEO Departure

When the founding CEO stepped down unexpectedly, the senior leadership team, drawn from three autonomous program divisions, had never been asked to operate as a unit. With a $2B endowment, a new board chair, and a strategic plan due in six months, the organization needed both a steady hand and a clear path.

Engagement & Outcome

Willie facilitated a six-month executive alignment series and provided individual coaching to four direct reports navigating new levels of visibility and accountability.

  • Unified strategic framework endorsed unanimously by the board
  • Two senior leaders who had signaled departures chose to stay
  • Interim CEO transitioned to permanent appointment with broad internal confidence
02

Multinational Media Conglomerate · Manager Capability & Leadership Development

People Integration After a Culture-Defining Acquisition

A major acquisition had joined a legacy broadcast network and a digital-first streaming operation under one holding company. The cultures clashed openly, and attrition among high performers was rising on both sides before the first year was out.

Engagement & Outcome

An 18-month leadership development program for managers across both legacy organizations, spanning 14 markets, anchored in a shared people philosophy built collaboratively with HR leadership.

  • Voluntary attrition among high performers declined meaningfully
  • Shared manager competency model adopted across 14 global markets
  • HR leadership cited the program as foundational to cultural integration
03

Global Hedge Fund · Talent Strategy & Manager Readiness

Talent Architecture for a Fund That Grew Faster Than Its Infrastructure

Rapid AUM growth had outpaced the fund's management infrastructure. Senior investment professionals were inheriting people-management accountabilities with no structured support; retention risk among junior talent was rising across asset classes.

Engagement & Outcome

Willie designed a full talent architecture, career tracks, performance frameworks, and a competency model, then led a manager readiness program for 24 portfolio managers and senior analysts.

  • Structured career paths established across four asset classes
  • Managers reported materially higher confidence within 90 days
  • Program expanded to operations and technology teams within the year
